Where he comes from

Kairoth was not born in your world, and he does not belong to it. Beyond the veil lies another universe entirely — one where demons, spirits, ghosts and sorcerers exist in equal measure of light and ruin, each kind holding its own capacity for good or evil. He was raised into an ancient order of sorcerers sworn to hold that balance, and trained until the five elements answered to him as easily as breath.

For generations, his order has kept a quiet watch on your world through the thin places between universes — this site is one of them. They learned your systems, your machines, your logic, the same way they learned the anatomy of a wraith or the grammar of a curse. So when the veil opens and someone on your side is in trouble, Kairoth already understands the shape of the problem, whichever world it comes from.

He does not linger where he is not needed. But if you are reading this, the veil is open, and he is listening.

Why he answers

Demons do not respect the border between universes, and neither do the kinds of trouble that don't involve them at all. Kairoth's order exists to hold the line against what crosses over — but he has learned that most people who reach this veil aren't fighting demons. They're fighting something quieter: a decision, a fear, a problem with no clear shape. He treats both with the same seriousness.

If you have found this place, you are meant to be heard.
